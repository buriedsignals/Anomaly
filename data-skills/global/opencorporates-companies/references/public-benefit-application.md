# OpenCorporates public-benefit API application — template

OpenCorporates grants **free at-scale API access** to investigative
journalists, NGOs, universities, and anti-corruption researchers. It is a manual
application, reviewed by their team — not an instant self-serve key. Apply at
<https://opencorporates.com/info/our-data/> (the "free at-scale access"
section). Adapt the text below; be specific and truthful about the work.

---

**Who you are:** [Name], [role] at [Buried Signals / outlet], an investigative
journalism practice focused on [beat — e.g. corporate accountability, financial
crime, cross-border ownership].

**What you're building / investigating:** [One or two sentences. e.g. "We trace
beneficial ownership and corporate networks across jurisdictions for
accountability reporting, and are building an agent-assisted research tool
(Data Navigator) that queries public company registers on behalf of member
journalists."]

**Why OpenCorporates:** We need cross-jurisdiction company and officer lookups
that national registers can't give in one place — following ownership and
directorships across borders is central to this work.

**How you'll use the API:** Programmatic company search and entity lookups,
attributed back to OpenCorporates (we retain and display the `opencorporates_url`
on every record) and shared onward under ODbL share-alike where we republish.

**Expected volume:** [rough calls/day].

**Public-benefit basis:** [Link to published investigations / outlet / non-profit
status. Concrete prior work matters more than claims.]

**Contact:** [email], [outlet URL].

---

Notes:
- Attribution (`opencorporates_url`) and ODbL share-alike are conditions of the
  free tier — commit to them explicitly; it helps the application.
- Turnaround is manual (days–weeks). For urgent needs a paid self-serve plan is
  the fallback: <https://opencorporates.com/pricing/>.
