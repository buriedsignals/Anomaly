# Case-law query guide

## Research loop

1. State the legal proposition or factual issue being researched.
2. Choose keyword/operator or semantic search deliberately and preserve it.
3. Decide whether published-only scope is appropriate; name every additional status.
4. Use court and date constraints where supported by a reviewed search URL.
5. Treat results as candidates. Open the linked opinion and read the relevant
   holding, procedural posture, status, and subsequent treatment.
6. Record cluster ID, nested opinion ID/type, court, date, citation, and URL.

## Identifier lookup

Use `cluster_id` for one CourtListener opinion cluster and `docket_id` for
opinions indexed against a docket. These are provider identifiers, not official
citations. A search hit still needs source reading.

## Reporting checklist

- Exact query and whether semantic search was used.
- Published-only or explicitly broadened status scope.
- Court, filing date, citation, cluster ID, and nested opinion type.
- Passage checked against the complete linked opinion.
- Whether later authority, contrary cases, or jurisdictional limits were checked.
- Coverage and no-result limitations stated without a universal conclusion.
