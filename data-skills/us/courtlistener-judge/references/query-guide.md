# Judicial-person query guide

## Discovery and resolution

1. Search the name with `q` to discover aliases and candidates.
2. Use exact `name_first`/`name_last` database filtering where helpful.
3. Compare middle/suffix names, birth details, education, FJC ID, positions,
   jurisdiction, and independent official biographies.
4. Check `is_alias_of`; do not count an alias as a second person.
5. Retrieve the exact person ID and follow position/source links as needed.

## Sensitive fields

Always carry date granularity with a birth/death date. Describe race and gender
as CourtListener-coded/inferred only when editorially necessary, and verify with
an appropriate primary/self-identification source before asserting them.

Political affiliations can have source codes and date ranges; they do not
automatically describe current party registration, ideology, or judicial conduct.

## Reporting checklist

- Canonical CourtListener person ID and alias resolution.
- Exact name components, suffix, and corroborating identifiers.
- Date plus granularity, never the placeholder date alone.
- Full position evidence rather than a link count.
- Source/retrieval date and contradictions.
- Demographic provenance and uncertainty stated explicitly.
