# Federal award query guide

## Make the search reproducible

1. Pick one award group and explain why.
2. Use a fixed fiscal year or explicit start/end dates.
3. Prefer `recipient` for identity-oriented searches; use `keyword` for broader
   award description/discovery and `award_id` for exact known IDs.
4. Preserve page, sort, order, returned `query_window`, and provider messages.
5. Open the award profile and resolve recipient UEI, agency, dates, and ID.

## Follow money at the correct level

Award-level amount summarizes the prime award under provider rules. For a claim
about money newly obligated, modified, outlaid, invoiced, paid, or passed to a
subrecipient, query the corresponding transaction/outlay/subaward evidence.

## Reporting checklist

- Award group and exact provider codes.
- Federal FY dates or explicit date window.
- Keyword/recipient/award-ID semantics.
- Award ID, generated internal ID, UEI, agency, and recipient resolution.
- Amount field named precisely; obligations/outlays/payments not conflated.
- Page, sort/order, retrieval date, corrections/messages, and no-result limits.
